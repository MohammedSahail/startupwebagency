Terms of Engagement

Last updated: November 2026 • Status: Marked for Owner / Legal Review Prior to Publication

1. Professional Services & Scoping

Start-Up Web Agency provides design, engineering, and digital consultation services. All projects are governed by an individual Statement of Work (SOW) specifying deliverables, milestones, and technical requirements agreed upon between the client and agency leadership (Mohammed Sahail & Nooman Pasha).

2. Intellectual Property & Code Ownership

Upon receipt of final payment in full according to the project agreement, the client receives full ownership of all custom design deliverables and project source code created specifically for their engagement, excluding pre-existing open-source web standards (HTML5/CSS3/JavaScript).

3. Project Timelines & Collaboration

Estimated milestones depend on mutual adherence to schedules and timely feedback. While we strive to meet targeted release dates, external dependencies, client-requested scope modifications, or delayed feedback will adjust milestone dates accordingly.

4. Warranties & Limitations

We craft all codebases to the highest native web standards and test thoroughly across standard viewports. However, we do not guarantee specific commercial outcomes, search engine ranking positions, or conversion rates, as these depend on broader market factors beyond design and development.
