Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026 • Status: Marked for Owner / Legal Review Prior to Publication

1. Information We Collect

Start-Up Web Agency operates a privacy-first, static digital platform. We do not use third-party behavioral advertising trackers, advertising pixels, or cross-site fingerprinting scripts. When you voluntarily contact us via our project planner form or direct email (startupwebagency@gmail.com), you provide information such as your name, email address, phone number, organization name, and project requirements.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information you transmit is used solely by agency leadership—Mohammed Sahail (Founder & CEO) and Nooman Pasha (Co-founder & Managing Director)—to:

  • Evaluate project suitability, feasibility, and technical requirements.
  • Communicate directly regarding project proposals, milestones, and deliverables.
  • Fulfill professional contractual and accounting obligations.

3. Cookies & Local Storage

This website uses standard vanilla JavaScript without persistent advertising cookies. Any local state stored (such as UI preferences) remains strictly on your device.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or project data to third-party marketing brokers. Data is disclosed only when required by applicable law or with your explicit consent during client project deployment.

5. Contacting the Agency

For privacy inquiries, data access requests, or corrections, contact our founders directly at:
Email: startupwebagency@gmail.com
